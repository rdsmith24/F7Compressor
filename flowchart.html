States & Relay Actions
StateRUN relay PG4Meaning
IDLE OFF Motor stopped. Waiting for operator.
STARTING ON Motor energised. Oil switch must close within oil_pressure_delay_s.
RUNNING ON Normal operation. Monitors oil & pressure thresholds.
STOPPING OFF Motor de-energised. Waiting for pressure to fall to cut-in.
FAULT OFF Latched fault. Operator must acknowledge alarm and press RESET.
E-STOP OFF Relay cut immediately. NC contact must physically re-close before reset allowed.
Pressure Thresholds (configurable in Settings)
SettingDefaultMeaning
pressure_min_psi 80 PSI Low pressure alarm: set if psi is still below this when the oil delay expires
pressure_max_psi 100 PSI Cut-out: normal stop when psi reaches this (no alarm)
pressure_span_psi 10 PSI Hysteresis band — cut-in = max − span (90 PSI default)
pressure_high_alarm_psi 110 PSI Controlled stop + HIGH_PRESSURE alarm (auto-clears when psi drops)
pressure_high_high_psi 120 PSI Emergency shutdown → FAULT + HIGH_HIGH_PRES (RUNNING or STOPPING)
oil_pressure_delay_s 5 s Grace period for oil pressure to build after motor start
auto_restart_en ON STOPPING→STARTING (duty cycle) vs STOPPING→IDLE (manual restart)
Alarm Bitmask
AlarmBitSet whenCleared by
ALARM_OIL_TIMEOUT 0x01 Oil switch didn't close within oil_pressure_delay_s FAULT reset or enter IDLE
ALARM_PRESSURE_FAULT 0x02 ADC reading outside 4–20 mA window (wiring / sensor fault) FAULT reset or enter IDLE
ALARM_ESTOP 0x04 E-stop NC contact opened (PC2 HIGH) NC contact re-closes + RESET
ALARM_LOW_PRESSURE 0x08 Discharge psi < pressure_min_psi when oil delay expires FAULT reset or enter IDLE
ALARM_HIGH_PRESSURE 0x10 Discharge psi ≥ pressure_high_alarm_psi while RUNNING Auto-cleared when psi drops back below high alarm threshold
ALARM_HIGH_HIGH_PRES 0x20 Discharge psi ≥ pressure_high_high_psi (RUNNING or STOPPING) FAULT reset or enter IDLE
