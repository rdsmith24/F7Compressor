★ Morpho — solder header required
★ Morpho connectors CN11 / CN12 are unpopulated from factory. You must solder a male pin header to access pins CN12-P10 (FMC_D13 / PD8), CN11-P69 (FMC_D14 / PD9), and CN12-P65 (FMC_D15 / PD10). All other signals use the pre-populated Zio/Arduino headers (CN7–CN10). Do not connect CN1 (ST-Link USB) and J-Link simultaneously — they contend on SWD.

Notes

  • LCD pins 24, 25, 26 are not connected — leave open.
  • LCD pins 2, 3 are GND on the Waveshare board — tie to GND or leave open.
  • Do NOT connect CN1 (ST-Link USB) and J-Link simultaneously — they contend on SWD lines.
  • JP3 (T_NRST, 2-pin) must remain installed for J-Link reset control.
  • LCD_BL_ON() / LCD_BL_OFF() macros are ineffective — PC6 is in TIM3_CH1 AF2 mode. Backlight is controlled by PWM (enabled in firmware init).
  • RST (PG2) is held HIGH at startup; the ILI9341 library uses a software reset command (0x01) instead of toggling this pin.
  • After first flash, tune XPT_CAL_X_MIN/MAX, XPT_CAL_Y_MIN/MAX, XPT_SWAP_XY, XPT_FLIP_X/Y in BSP/Inc/xpt2046_spi.h to match your panel orientation.
  • Total signal connections: 27 wires (16 data + 4 FMC ctrl + 2 GPIO/PWM + 5 SPI touch + 1 VCC + 3 GND). LCD pins 24–26 NC; LCD pins 2–3 GND (optional).
